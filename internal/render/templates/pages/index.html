{{template "lcars-bar" "PillGauge"}}

A single value against a range, shown as a stadium pill with a gradient fill clipped to the value's position. Best for percentages, temperatures, and speeds where one number matters. Build with view.RenderGauge; its Frac (0–1) drives the fill and the colour reflects the level.

{{template "lcars-bar" "LevelMeter"}}

A single value as a segmented vertical gauge (VU-meter style): pill segments light from the bottom up, each showing its slice of a gradient so the colour tracks the level. Inline SVG, scales to any box. Build with view.RenderMeter(id, value, min, max, segments, gap, stops).
